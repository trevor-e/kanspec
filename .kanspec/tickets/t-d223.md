## Steps

§10's file-ownership map and the five no-collision rules (no new mod line, out.rs never grows, plan.rs frozen, ...) were merge-avoidance rules for the nine-agent build. They now dictate where code lives: shared helpers `facts`, `join`, `write_next`, `minted` sit in cmd/ticket.rs because their natural homes are frozen, and derive.rs mirrors scan.rs's `ticket_rev`/`note_sha` instead of importing them. Decide which rules still buy something (the closed KsError shapes arguably do) and rewrite §10 as steady-state guidance; then move the helpers to out.rs/plan.rs and delete the mirrors.
