rules --audit: don't flag a decision the moment its source proposal closes

## Steps

DESIGN.md's own example says "closed 80 days ago" — the heuristic needs an age (config knob, e.g. `[windows] decision_review_secs`, default ~90 days) and should count the proposal's shipped rules as references, not only open tickets.
